About

Each time a piece of Dross is created in an Ore Smelter there is a small chance for it to become A Face in the Dross instead. A Face in the Dross is a Top 10 Artifact for Blunt Power. A Face in the Dross can be stored in a Stockbin. Note: As A Face in the Dross is identical to Dross when Paving and Fertilizing it might be better to just use Dross for these activities instead.
